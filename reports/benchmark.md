# Benchmark: random incidents

30 scenarios (seed 0), 6 of them with no incident; 43 incidents in the others. Each incident hits a random segment of platform, country, channel and device (1-3 conditions) with a conversion or traffic change, down or up, in the week of Oct 2-8, on top of the usual decoys: a German holiday in the same week, payday, seasonality, aliases and 20 noise dimensions. Generated by `scripts/benchmark.py`.

A planted cause is **material** for a metric when its true impact is at least 2% of the metric's level in the incident week. Recall counts only the exact segment; a near miss is a coarser or finer segment containing or inside it. Precision counts accepted causes that are exactly a planted segment, material or not.

| `orders` | 25 | 16 (64%) | 2 | 7 | 65% (26 accepted) | 8 | 0/6 | 100% | n/a | 94% | 0.9 pp | 3 (max 10) | 2s |
